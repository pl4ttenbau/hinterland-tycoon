# W50 v4 photo sources

- **W 50 L Blau** by Darkone, 2 July 2005. [Source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:W_50_L_Blau.jpg) · [CC BY-SA 2.5](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.5/). Paint and headlamp crops were rectified, stripped of the original color cast, and graded. The IFA and W50 L marks are redrawn from the visible reference.
- **IFA W 50 L (side)** by Fiver, der Hellseher. [Source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:IFA_W_50_L_(side).jpg) · [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Canvas and painted-board crops were rectified and blended into the authored surfaces. Company lettering and registration marks were excluded.

The photo-derived atlas is offered under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/), a later version permitted by CC BY-SA 2.5 for adaptations. The pictured trucks are texture donors. The v3 geometry and model variant establish the asset's dimensions and fittings. IK 16-24 is an illustrative registration.
