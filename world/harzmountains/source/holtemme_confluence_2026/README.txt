Holtemme final approach and confluence correction — 26 November 2026

Saved two native Terrain3D regions. The Hasserode approach now runs west of the railway to a model-scale confluence at X2975/Z1330, west of Westerntor station. The old southeastern connecting branch was filled locally using surrounding bank heights, with tapered ends. Upstream Hasserode channel and the Zillierbach channel are retained.

No road/rail geometry, texture-control data, colour maps, or water surfaces changed. Water placement is for the user. The alignment follows the relationship shown by the period plan, fitted to the compressed scene, and is not a GPS-surveyed trace. Road crossings and banks still need an in-editor visual check; no bridges were added.

See alignment.json for the route and bed levels, report.json for measurements and native_verification.json for reload checks. Original binary regions are retained as .before files.
