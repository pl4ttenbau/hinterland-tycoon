# Gdańsk / Żuławy — industry candidates for a 1960s world

Research date: 7 November 2026. This is a historical shortlist, not a completed inventory or a placement file.

## Area and interpretation

The current world uses **18.660–19.213° E, 54.0465–54.370° N**, from `../jsondata/mapinfo.json`. Its original 1960 starting year has since been changed to **1968**, following the agreed Gdańsk neighbourhood additions; the initial research below retains its earlier/later distinctions. The emphasis here is the Żuławy hinterland, with Tczew and the eastern industrial edge of Gdańsk where they fall within the crop. Malbork's town centre and several factories are south of the crop.

Dates below describe the evidence found, rather than asserting that every postwar enterprise had an unchanged site, name and product range throughout 1960–1969. Current company addresses must not automatically be used as historical factory coordinates. Exact footprints and private sidings remain to be checked before placement.

**All cargo chains in the last column are proposed game adaptations.** They are plausible for the industry, but do not establish a documented shipment, rail siding, transport mode or precise 1960 production recipe.

## Main candidates within the world or at its immediate edge

| Industry / place | Period evidence and historical fit | Possible game role / cargo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Sugarworks — Nowy Staw** | Excellent anchor industry. Municipal history records the first postwar campaign in autumn **1946**, with employment exceeding 1,000 in 1950. The railway operator documents the factory's historic narrow-gauge connection and freight continuing until 1996. [Municipal history](https://www.nowystaw.pl/asp/historia,29), [railway history](https://kolejzulawska.pl/o-nas/historia/). | Beets, coal and limestone in; sugar, molasses and beet pulp out. Seasonal agricultural freight. |
| **Sugarworks — Stare Pole** | Excellent second anchor near the southeastern corner. The historic complex dates from **1880**. Industrial-railway research records the factory's operation of a former PKP **750 mm** network in **1958–1967**. These are railway dates, not the factory's entire lifespan. [National heritage register](https://zabytek.pl/pl/obiekty/g-237023), [Ariel Ciechański, industrial railway study, 2013](https://rcin.org.pl/Content/48222/PDF/WA51_63309_r2013-nr243_Prace-Geogr.pdf). | A second beet catchment, with sugar production and animal-feed by-products. Particularly relevant to narrow gauge. |
| **Maltings — Nowy Staw** | Municipal history records the postwar restart in **1946**. A strong period candidate; the exact 1960 capacity and customer breweries have not been established. [Municipal history](https://www.nowystaw.pl/asp/historia,29). | Barley and fuel in; malt out to an off-map brewery or trade connection. |
| **Brickworks — Nowy Staw** | Reopened in **July 1948**, according to the municipality. Good postwar candidate; a separate source for its exact 1960 operating status and clay-pit arrangement is still desirable. [Municipal history](https://www.nowystaw.pl/asp/historia,29). | Local clay and imported fuel in; bricks out to towns and construction sites. |
| **Cooperative dairy — Nowy Staw** | Reopened in **April 1947**. Suitable postwar dairy candidate; exact 1960 product mix remains unverified. [Municipal history](https://www.nowystaw.pl/asp/historia,29). | Milk collection and dairy products; packaging and fuel deliveries. |
| **District dairy cooperative, later Maluta — Nowy Dwór Gdański** | Company history explicitly dates the cooperative to **1963**. Suitable for the decade, but it should not automatically appear under that organization in a strict 1960 start. Its current Warszawska 41 address does not prove the complete 1963 footprint. [Company history](https://maluta.pl/pl/o-firmie/). | Milk from surrounding farms; butter, cheese and other dairy products for towns/export. |
| **Gas-meter factory — Tczew** | **Pomorska Fabryka Gazomierzy** is documented for **1948–1972**, including a Piaskowa site from **1954**. The Predom-Metrix names belong to later reorganizations; do not label the 1960 plant as the later household-appliance enterprise. [State Archive collection](https://www.szukajwarchiwach.gov.pl/en/zespol/-/zespol/151998), [municipal library chronology and 1963 photograph](https://skarbnica.tczew.pl/29495/tczew-ochotnicza-straz-pozarna-miasta-tczewa-zawody-powiatowe-zawody-strazackie-lata-60-xx-wieku/). | Metal parts in; gas meters / manufactured goods out. A medium engineering works. |
| **Motor-vehicle components and gearbox works — Tczew** | Museum history documents a foundry expansion and tractor-parts production in **1952**, complete gearboxes in the late **1950s–1960s**, and the **POLMO name from 1968**. Use Zakłady Sprzętu Motoryzacyjnego for the earlier period. The museum's address is an exhibition venue, not proof of the factory location. [Fabryka Sztuk historical exhibition](https://fabrykasztuk.tczew.pl/historia-rozwoju-techniki-od-muscate-do-eaton-wystawa/). | Steel and castings in; machinery parts and gearboxes out. One substantial engineering employer. |
| **Yeast / starch works — Tczew** | Municipal library chronology lists yeast and starch production in **1945–1962**, then a yeast factory within the Starogard Polmos organization in **1962–1991**. The site was formerly a sugarworks, but that earlier use must not be carried into 1960. [Library chronology](https://skarbnica.tczew.pl/43357/tczew-stare-miasto-fragment-ulicy-zamkowej-widok-od-strony-skrzyzowania-ulic-zamkowej-i-okrzei-lata-90-xx-wieku-3/). | Molasses to yeast is an attractive game connection with the sugarworks; the specific historical supplier is unverified. Starch can be an additional early-period product. |
| **Town gasworks — Tczew** | Local historical account documents postwar operation, production figures for **1964**, and the end of coal carbonization in **1974**. [Historical account](https://www.dawnytczew.pl/de/gospodarka/329-gazownia.html). | Coal consumer; town gas, coke and tar as possible outputs. |
| **River shipyard — Górki Zachodnie, Gdańsk** | The predecessor of **Stocznia Wisła** resumed repairs in **1946**, became a state enterprise in **1950**, and received the Wisła name in **1967**. Official coordinates **54.35278° N, 18.78056° E** place the yard inside our crop. [Company history and location](https://stoczniawisla.pl/about-us,2,en). | Steel, timber, engines and equipment to shipbuilding / repair. A waterfront industry of manageable scale. |
| **Auxiliary river repair yards — Przegalina and Tczew** | The State Archive's description of Gdańska Stocznia Rzeczna lists auxiliary yards at these places, alongside the main Górki yard. Exact 1960 footprints, division of work and continued operation of each subsidiary need individual checking. [State Archive company history](https://www.szukajwarchiwach.gov.pl/en/zespol/-/zespol/152566). | Smaller repair landings: timber, steel, machinery and maintenance supplies. Candidates rather than fully dated placements. |
| **Narrow-gauge workshops — Nowy Dwór Gdański** | The operator records the station and workshops being expanded around **1955–1956**, taking over Lisewo's central role. Particularly good for a 1960 start. [Operator history](https://kolejzulawska.pl/o-nas/historia/). | Coal, spare parts, lubricants and repair materials; a railway maintenance destination. |
| **Railway depot / wagon facilities — Zajączkowo Tczewskie** | The municipal library holds a **1950–1980 railway technical club chronicle**, covering the Tczew junction and locomotive/wagon facilities. A period-supported railway-service candidate; identify the relevant historical buildings before placing it. [Digitized chronicle](https://skarbnica.tczew.pl/45325/klub-techniki-i-racjonalizacji-wezla-kolejowego-tczew-kronika-z-lat-1950-1980/). | Coal, spare parts and railway repair services; standard-gauge freight and maintenance. |
| **Railway repair works, ZNTK — Gdańsk Przeróbka** | City encyclopedia dates the ZNTK organization to **1952**, following the postwar railway workshops. A sizeable industry at the northwestern edge; check which historical buildings fit the crop before committing to a full complex. [Przeróbka history](https://gdansk.gedanopedia.pl/gdansk/?title=PRZER%C3%93BKA). | Steel, components and equipment to rolling-stock repair. Standard-gauge industry. |
| **Agricultural machine service — Sztutowo** | Municipality records the opening of a **Spółdzielczy Ośrodek Maszynowy on 1 September 1948**. A good small rural service-site candidate; the same organization and premises in 1960 are not independently confirmed. [Municipal chronology](https://www.sztutowo.pl/nasza-gmina/historia/). | Fuel, spare parts and machinery; repairs for surrounding farms. |
| **Fishing cooperative / landing and collection facilities — Stegna coast** | Municipal chronology records **Spółdzielnia Rybacka Mierzeja in Stegna in 1951**, formed from Zalew and Fala. This supports fishing activity, not a demonstrated large cannery or a private rail siding. [Municipal chronology](https://www.sztutowo.pl/nasza-gmina/historia/). | Small fish landing/collection destination; fish to local towns or an external processor. |
| **Milk collection point — Łaszka** | Municipal chronology records opening a milk collection point in **1950**. A smaller and better documented alternative to inventing a dairy factory in every village. [Municipal chronology](https://www.sztutowo.pl/nasza-gmina/historia/). | Local farm milk to a regional dairy. |
| **State farms / agricultural depots — scattered through Żuławy** | Archival records cover farms including **Koszwały, Krzywe Koło, Cyganek, Nowa Cerkiew and Orłowo** during the late 1950s and 1960s. The archive establishes enterprises/records, not an individual silo or loading ramp at each modern farmyard. [State Archive inventory](https://www.szukajwarchiwach.gov.pl/seria?_Seria_delta=100&_Seria_id_serii=676438&_Seria_nameofjsp=jednostki&_Seria_resetCur=false&p_p_id=Seria&p_p_lifecycle=0&p_p_mode=view&p_p_state=normal). | Several distributed grain, beet, livestock and milk producers; fertilizer, fuel and machinery consumers. |

## Infrastructure and a lead requiring another check

- **Chłodniewo drainage pumping station:** built in **1930**, damaged in 1945 and returned to service during postwar drainage recovery. Its location is approximately **54.28315° N, 19.11575° E**, within the crop. This is landscape-defining infrastructure rather than a major freight producer. Do not assume a coal-fired 1960 plant. [Regional tourism account](https://pomorskie.travel/en/punkty-poi/stacja-pomp-chlodniewo/).
- **Rybina dairy:** an indexed **1959 Dziennik Bałtycki** tender notice concerns completion of building work and sewerage for an expanded dairy, with a proposed completion deadline of **31 January 1960**. It supports an existing dairy/expansion project, not proof that the contract was completed on time. The scan server was intermittently unavailable during research; re-open the notice before using its wording for a definitive chronology. [Newspaper scan](https://bibliotekacyfrowa.eu/Content/14100/16413-0001.pdf).

## Earlier industries and nearby trading partners

- **Stobbe / Machandel distillery, Nowy Dwór Gdański:** a distinctive local business founded in **1776**, ending at its original location in **1945**. Suitable for an earlier scenario or industrial remains. A later revival of the brand abroad does not establish a postwar distillery here. [Regional history](https://pomorskie.travel/en/articles/nowy-dwor-gdanski-the-capital-of-the-low-lands/), [waterways heritage account](https://petla-zulaw.pl/obiekty/warto-zobaczyc/533%2Cstobbes-machandel.html).
- **Cedry Wielkie sugarworks:** historical sugar-railway origin, but the factory closed in **1916**. Its early railway had the unusual **780 mm** gauge; surviving remnants of that system disappeared in the 1950s. Do not recreate it as an operating 1960 sugarworks. [Industrial railway study](https://rcin.org.pl/Content/48222/PDF/WA51_63309_r2013-nr243_Prace-Geogr.pdf), [operator history](https://kolejzulawska.pl/o-nas/historia/).
- **Lisewo sugarworks:** documented as an early beet-railway origin, with a **750 mm line opened in 1894**. Operation of the factory itself in 1960 has not been established here; keep it as a historical lead. [Operator history](https://kolejzulawska.pl/o-nas/historia/).
- **Malbork sugarworks:** founded in **1881**, restarted after the war, with the first postwar campaign in **1947**. An important period trading partner, but the factory is south of our **54.0465° N** boundary, around **54.034° N**. It should not be counted as a third sugarworks inside the current crop. [Malbork museum history](https://muzeum.malbork.pl/p%2C128%2Cpunkt-z-widokiem-na-ul-sikorskiego), [location reference](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Sugar_refinery_in_Malbork).
- **Organika, Malbork:** company's **65th anniversary in 2026** places its foundation around **1961**. Its Kałdowo site is just south of our crop, around **54.039° N**. Its current polyurethane product range is not proof of its exact opening-year production. [Company anniversary](https://organika.pl/w-2026-roku-firma-organika-obchodzi-jubileusz-65-lecie-dzialalnosci/).
- **Malma pasta factory, Malbork:** municipal history dates opening to **1962**. A useful nearby food-industry partner. Exact original footprint relative to the southern boundary remains to be checked. [Municipal history](https://urzad.malbork.pl/historia.html).

## Suggested first selection for this world

For a rural 1960 start, prioritize **Nowy Staw and Stare Pole sugarworks**, several **farm/beet loading sites**, **Nowy Staw maltings**, one or two **dairy/collection sites**, the **Nowy Staw brickworks** pending a 1960 operation check, and **Nowy Dwór railway workshops**. Add a few Tczew engineering/food sites and one river repair yard for variety. Introduce the Nowy Dwór district dairy organization from **1963** and the refinery from **1975** if the scenario advances in time.

Coal, fertilizer and building-material depots are useful complementary game destinations: these traffic types are documented in the regional railway history, although individual depot locations still need research. A nearby narrow-gauge line is not by itself proof that every listed factory had its own siding. The cross-Vistula railway ferry had already closed in **1955–1956**, which matters for a strict 1960 routing plan. [Operator history](https://kolejzulawska.pl/o-nas/historia/).

No world scenes, industry nodes or terrain were changed by this research.
