# Which parts of Gdańsk are in world A?

Research: 7 November 2026. Crop: **18.660–19.213° E, 54.0465–54.370° N**. No world nodes or terrain changed by this research.

Subsequent placement: **Gdańsk–Stogi, Gdańsk–Przeróbka, Gdańsk–Rudno (Długie Ogrody), and Gdańsk–Górki Zachodnie** are now towns 21–24 in `towns_gdansk.tscn`, added at the user's request. The first three use the major setting; Górki uses minor. These are gameplay classifications, not historical population estimates. The map default year is now **1968**. Centres use mapped residential-street anchors, with heights sampled from the current terrain. The original twenty towns are retained. See [placement manifest](../../source/gdansk_town_additions.json) and [validation](../../source/gdansk_town_additions_validation.json). No residences, roads, trams or terrain were generated by this placement pass.

## Coverage

I intersected the city's public district polygons with the crop. These are **modern administrative areas**, used to identify locations; they are not a reconstruction of 1960 boundaries. Percentages include administrative water areas where applicable and do not indicate the fraction of residents captured. Source: [City of Gdańsk open boundary dataset](https://ckan.multimediagdansk.pl/dataset/granice-dzielnic-w-gdansku). Source ZIP is retained here; calculation results are in [coverage.json](coverage.json). Population attributes from the modern dataset were not used.

| Modern district / historical locality | Approximate share of modern district area in crop | Interpretation for a 1960s map |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| **Stogi / Heubude** | 26% | Southern residential neighbourhood and much of its core; northern edge, beach and most northern port/forest territory cut off. Modern block construction began in **1963**. A strict 1960 version should emphasize older low-rise settlement. |
| **Przeróbka / Troyl** | 31% | Southern residential/industrial part, including the railway works and Siennicka neighbourhood; most northern port territory lies outside. The **Bajka estate began in the mid-1960s**, with a Siennicka block handed over in July 1965. |
| **Śródmieście — eastern part** | 39% | Długie Ogrody, Rudno, Angielska/Sienna Grobla and eastern Dolne Miasto areas; some localities are clipped by the western boundary. Main Gdańsk centre lies west of the crop. Rudno's Obrońców Poczty Polskiej estate was built **1959–1962**. |
| **Krakowiec–Górki Zachodnie** | 92% | Mostly captured: smaller waterfront settlements, shipyard/repair activity, gardens and woods. Górki gained a small two-storey shipyard-workers' estate in the **1950s**. |
| **Rudniki — including Błonia and Płonia areas** | 100% | Spacious, relatively sparse industrial/agricultural fringe. The refinery is a later, 1975 transformation already accepted for this game scenario. Rudno and Rudniki are different places despite confusing postwar naming. |
| **Olszynka** | 94% | Nearly all: low houses, former farmsteads, drainage ditches, fields and allotments rather than a dense urban estate. |
| **Wyspa Sobieszewska** | Over 99% | Almost the whole island: Sobieszewo, Świbno, Górki Wschodnie, Przegalina and smaller settlements. **Not part of Gdańsk in the 1960s**; incorporated on **1 December 1973**. |
| **Orunia–Św. Wojciech–Lipce** | 22% | Eastern river/field fringe; the principal urban settlements lie west of our boundary. |
| **Młyniska** | 10% | A small northeastern industrial/island fringe, not the main residential district. |

The parts of today's city with the largest area inside our crop are **Wyspa Sobieszewska, Rudniki, Krakowiec–Górki Zachodnie and Olszynka**. Residential importance is different: the main urban concentrations to represent are **Stogi, southern Przeróbka, and eastern Śródmieście/Rudno**. This is a qualitative modelling priority, not a verified 1960 district-population ranking. Wrzeszcz, Oliwa and Przymorze are outside the crop.

For scale, a retrospective engineering history discussing the Siennicki bridge around its **1959 assessment / 1961 repair design** gives approximately **8,000 residents for the wider Stogi-side area**, describing a region that includes the railway works now associated with Przeróbka. Do not assign that number to the modern Stogi district alone or add it to a separate Przeróbka total. The **1970 census counted 2,850 permanent residents across the entire Sobieszewo island**—large in area, modest in population.

Sources: [Stogi history](https://gdansk.gedanopedia.pl/gdansk/?title=STOGI), [Przeróbka history](https://gdansk.gedanopedia.pl/gdansk/?title=PRZER%C3%93BKA), [Rudno history and housing](https://gdansk.gedanopedia.pl/?title=RUDNO), [Olszynka history](https://gdansk.gedanopedia.pl/gdansk/?title=OLSZYNKA), [Górki Zachodnie history](https://gdansk.gedanopedia.pl/gdansk/?title=G%C3%93RKI_ZACHODNIE), [island incorporation and 1970 population](https://gdansk.gedanopedia.pl/gdansk/?title=WYSPA_SOBIESZEWSKA), [Siennicki bridge engineering history, pp. 17–18](https://pom.piib.org.pl/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Pomorski-Inzynier-nr-44.pdf).

## Four photo references

These are linked reference images, not generated illustrations. Dates were checked against the archive captions or the caption printed on the published photo. The selection spans **1927–1972**; the older image is explicitly an earlier transport reference, not evidence for an unchanged 1960 streetscape. Copyright/credit remains with the stated source; the photographs have not been relicensed as game assets.

### 1. Rudno / eastern Śródmieście — 1960

Obrońców Poczty Polskiej estate under construction around Głęboka, Mostek and Wygon. Useful for the mix of lower slab blocks and taller point blocks appearing at the edge of the old city. This is a construction scene, not the completed estate in 1960. [Source and dated caption: Gedanopedia](https://gdansk.gedanopedia.pl/?title=RUDNO).

![Rudno housing under construction in 1960](https://gdansk.gedanopedia.pl/images/d/df/4_Rudno.jpg)

### 2. Stogi, Stryjewskiego — January 1972

Street scene with a public telephone queue, small-scale streetscape and tram infrastructure. A little later than the main target period, useful for everyday atmosphere. **Zbigniew Kosycarz / KFP**, published in the album preview carried by [Fakt](https://www.fakt.pl/wydarzenia/polska/trojmiasto/kosycarz-nowy-album-znanych-fotografow-promocja-ksiazki-juz-w-weekend/hrtsn4s). The printed caption gives the date and street.

![Stogi, Stryjewskiego, January 1972 — Zbigniew Kosycarz / KFP](https://ocdn.eu/pulscms-transforms/1/YvWk9kuTURBXy8yOTM2MWU1OC0zN2QzLTQzZDQtYTI2NC1kNDE1ZTI5ZmY1MjAuanBlZ5GTAs0DSM0CTt4AAqEwAaExAQ)

### 3. Sobieszewo–Wiślinka ferry — 22 August 1965

Particularly relevant to our rural/waterfront map: small ferry, cars, passengers and the settlement on the opposite bank. It shows the **ferry**, not the later pontoon bridge. **Zbigniew Kosycarz / KFP**, published in the same [album preview](https://www.fakt.pl/wydarzenia/polska/trojmiasto/kosycarz-nowy-album-znanych-fotografow-promocja-ksiazki-juz-w-weekend/hrtsn4s). Date and crossing are printed on the image.

![Wiślinka–Sobieszewo ferry, 22 August 1965 — Zbigniew Kosycarz / KFP](https://ocdn.eu/pulscms-transforms/1/cAak9kuTURBXy80ZGExOGVlYy1kNjEzLTQ4NzktODViOC0zZmJlYTkxNmJmYWQuanBlZ5GTAs0DSM0CXt4AAqEwAaExAQ)

### 4. Przeróbka, Lenartowicza railway overbridge — 1927

Earlier transport reference showing the railway crossing above tram line 4 toward Stogi. The archive says this viaduct survived until dismantling in 2016, but the photo itself does not establish the appearance of the surrounding area in 1960. [Source and dated caption: Muzeum Polski](https://muzeumpolski.pl/resource/1691_wiadukt-kolejowy-na-przerobce.html).
