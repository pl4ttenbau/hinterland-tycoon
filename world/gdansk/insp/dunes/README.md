# Dune ridge reference photographs

`exposed_dune_ridge_photo5.jpg` is the original embedded JPEG extracted without editing from Photo 5 on PDF page 12 (printed page 11) of the Pomeranian landscape audit, landscape 22-313.53-24, **Lasy nadmorskie w rejonie Jantaru**, municipalities Stegna and Sztutowo. The Polish caption describes a fragment of dune ridge exposed through forestry. The exact camera coordinates and slope orientation are not stated; do not present it as a verified photograph of a particular Stegna street or the exact in-game cursor position.

Source: https://www.sztutowo.pl/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/22-313.53-24-Plaze-Mierzei-Wislanej-czesc-srodkowa.pdf

The original report is retained as `jantar_coastal_forests_landscape_report.pdf`. No map or terrain files were changed.
